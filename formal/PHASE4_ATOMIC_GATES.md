## Scope of the three new modules

### 1. StieltjesAtomicGaps.lean — where graph completion must occur

For each monotone right-continuous edge-dollar cumulative flow `Z_e`, the true dollar atom is

`a_e(t) = Z_e(t) - Z_e(t-) = μ_e({t}) ≥ 0`.

The scaled common variation clock `u(t)=t+λΣ_e Z_e(t)` has a **left** and a **right** limit at any atomic timestamp. The candidate Lean proofs identify its skipped width exactly:

`u(t)-u(t-) = λ Σ_e a_e(t)`.

They also define the affine *accounting* curve `Z_e(t-)+r a_e(t)`, `0≤r≤1`, with the correct endpoints, monotonicity and `λ·ΔZ_e≤Δu` per-edge increment bound. The physical time remains fixed at t.

**Important restriction:** proportional affine interpolation is an accounting reference, not a claim that simultaneous trading of all jumping edges respects the manuscript's actual transaction order or sale inventory. The physical-price path must be generated by actual marginal execution flows separately, not by interpolating endpoint prices.

There are at most countably many timestamps with a nonzero atom in a finite paired-dollar network (using the bundled Mathlib countability theorem for jumps of monotone functions). **A countable jump set is not the same as finitely many pulses** and does not handle the singular-continuous nonatomic component.

### 2. AtomicFlowCompletion.lean — physical marginal settlement at one true atom

`AtomicEdgeCompletion` couples a typed physical marginal-dollar pulse `FlowCompletedPulse` to one particular edge e and its exact Stieltjes singleton-dollar mass `μ_e({t})`.

For each supplied physically feasible pulse the intended formal results establish:

- the endpoint nonlinear normalized-price displacement `ΔΦ_i = B_{ie} μ_e({t})` from the actual price ODE + FTC;
- physical signed-dollar ledger increment `ΔX_i=B_{ie} μ_e({t})` from the actual dollar settlement ODE;
- positive prices and no-short executed-share inventory at **every internal operational point**;
- aggregate transformed potential conservation.

A pulse is supplied as proof-bearing data. **No proof states that arbitrary masses admit a positive-price/no-short physical solution.** That physical feasibility matters for M1–M5 and cannot be discarded.

### 3. OrderedAtomicCompletion.lean — real execution order at simultaneous jumps

`OrderedAtomicJumpTrace` records a finite, ordered list of physically flow-completed edge pulses at the SAME physical time t. Every join matches physical time, price vector, share holdings and per-asset signed-dollar ledger. A `Finset κ` records used edges, and the constructor rejects a second completion of an already used edge.

The intended invariant is: at every partial trace and for every asset, its **actual** dollar-ledger increment equals the incidence-weighted Stieltjes singleton-dollar masses of exactly those edges already completed. Once the used set covers `Finset.univ`, each edge has been completed precisely once and the final per-asset cash ledger equals the input atomic execution matrix. The aggregate coordinate potential remains invariant through the entire feasible ordered trace. The construction is also intended to map into `PhysicalMarketReachable` and reuse Phase 2 barrier results.

This is **not** a generic existence theorem for a trace of any simultaneous co-jump. If the ordered pulse sequence violates positivity or inventory at an internal step, it cannot inhabit the typed trace. Different feasible orders need not share the same intermediate prices and inventory profiles.

## Broader mathematical obligations at this checkpoint

These obligations concern the historical general-BV route. The [direct-paper route](DIRECT_PAPER_PROOF_ROUTE.md) distinguishes the manuscript's finite-pulse scope from optional countably atomic extensions.

1. **Full clock inverse/completed graph:** prove a globally defined Lipschitz time and edge-dollar graph over every clock gap, with order-preserving internal execution, connecting continuous time and all atoms. The current clock inverse is only defined on the actual (possibly discontinuous) clock image.
2. **A.e. physical solution for the full BV class:** carry the non-atomic absolute-continuous and singular-continuous components in one common operational graph, and derive the physical price/share/ledger solution with positive prices/no-short inventory. A measure-level dollar ledger alone does not prove nonlinear price response.
3. **Unconditional analytical FTC:** show `J(u)=exp(ρt(u)) Σ_i Φ(S_i(u))` is locally absolutely continuous, derive its a.e. derivative and prove the **endpoint FTC** using the pinned Lean/Mathlib environment. Phase 3's conditional FTC lemma is still not the full result.
4. **Original-model inclusion:** verify every M1–M5-admissible finite-variation path has a faithful representation by the constructed graph with terminal per-asset ledger closure, and prove elasticity only on the attainable price set suffices, including the log/Jensen part of Theorem 3.2.
5. **Separate research obligations:** other numbered paper results and the strict interval certificate of Proposition 7.6 remain outside these constructions.

## Verification qualifications

- Price-coordinate conservation requires derivation from marginal-price/share/dollar execution equations; treating it as an independent assumption would change the proof scope.
- A Lebesgue-a.e. derivative does not establish an endpoint integral identity without the necessary absolute-continuity argument.
- Individual theorem axiom audits and exact-source identities distinguish verified results from candidate constructions. Historical committed-file identities are recorded in `release/SHA256SUMS`.
