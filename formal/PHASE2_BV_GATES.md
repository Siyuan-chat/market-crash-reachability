## Formalized results and their exact scopes

1. **Arbitrary Stieltjes execution accounting.** `BoundedVariationLedger.lean` and `StieltjesExecution.lean` cover nonnegative directed executed-dollar increments, genuine Lebesgue–Stieltjes measures on (a,b], additivity, signed per-asset ledger increments, total aggregate cancellation, terminal per-asset closure equivalence, and closure under concatenating closed intervals. No classical trading-time derivative is required for **this accounting layer**.
2. **Actual nonlinear one-edge physical response under arbitrary Stieltjes clocks.** `StieltjesClockPulse.lean` starts with a physically solved marginal-dollar price/share/ledger ODE, then composes its execution parameter with a monotone right-continuous Stieltjes clock. It proves arbitrary subinterval `coordinate_increment` and `ledger_increment` from the physical ODE and FTC, positivity, no short sale, share bounded variation, and exact aggregate potential conservation. This accommodates singular-continuous and atomic **one-edge trade-only** clocks; an atom uses the full internal marginal flow rather than a block endpoint execution price.
3. **Multiple active edges together with recovery in operational time.** `OperationalClockMarket.lean` provides **one common operational parameter** `u`, with a nondecreasing physical-time clock `t(u)`, cumulative nondecreasing edge dollars `Z_e(u)`, their nonnegative derivatives, and *physical* price/share/ledger equations. Derived theorems include monotonicity of both clocks, the real nonlinear `coordinate_ode`, exact cancellation in the aggregate `potential_ode`, the variable-time integrating-factor comparison `exp(rho*t(u))*H(u)`, and the potential barrier `H>=0` when global `e>=1`. When `t'(u)=0`, simultaneously active trading edges exactly preserve aggregate transformed price potential.
4. **Attainable-price barrier in the joint clocked segment.** `OperationalClockAttainable.lean` uses the intermediate value theorem along each continuous operational-time price path starting at the common benchmark F. Every radial price between F and a visited price is actually visited. This gives a **one-segment physical multi-edge Theorem 3.1 analogue** under `e>=1` only at prices visited on that segment. A **one-segment 3.2 analogue** is also proved, but its `e>=2` assumption is still global over positive prices.
5. **Physical cumulative-dollar settlement and strong closure.** `OperationalClockLedger.lean` proves from the same rates that the physical per-asset ledger increment on any interval equals the incidence-weighted cumulative executed edge-dollar increment. It proves aggregate cancellation, the *strong* per-asset terminal closure iff condition when initial ledgers vanish, and edge-dollar bounded variation from nonnegative operational rates. These are derived from the physical and cumulative-edge ODEs.
6. **Finite physical market traces with all state fields matched.** `AdmissibleMarketPath.lean` and `AdmissiblePathBarrier.lean` now admit three kinds of steps: classical physical-time smooth segments, one-edge flow-completed pulses, and simultaneous multi-edge clocked segments. The finite trace matches *physical time, prices, shares and every per-asset dollar ledger* at each join, and requires terminal dollar closure asset by asset. The proved finite-path 3.1/3.2 analogues carry through the expanded class, but retain their **global** elasticity premises.
7. **Exact crisis semantics.** `CrisisParameters.lean` and `CrisisQuantile.lean` establish valid `k=ceil(alpha*N)`, actual benchmark log-loss identities, the finite-sample kth-largest/coverage-quantile characterization including ties, and threshold equivalences. These are not the remaining source of the full-BV physical-dynamics gap.

## Remaining mathematical bottleneck: arbitrary BV completed graph

A physically timed cumulative dollar control `Z_e(t)` can have absolutely continuous, singular-continuous, and atomic parts. Merely imposing a physical-time ODE almost everywhere **misses** the singular-continuous dollars. The correct next abstraction is a **joint flow-completed operational graph**.

- Choose positive reference scales for time and executed dollars (to avoid adding incompatible physical units). Form a normalized cumulative variation clock from physical time plus total executed-dollar variation. Resolve its jumps by explicitly ordered internal execution arcs, retaining the original order of marginal settlement rather than assuming independent edge flows commute for inventory/positivity.
- Prove existence of monotone **Lipschitz** completed-graph functions `t(u)` and `Z_e(u)`. They have derivatives almost everywhere, may have `t'(u)=0` on positive operational measure, and are not required to be differentiable at every `u`.
- Define a physical Carathéodory/absolutely-continuous solution to the common-clock price, share, and dollar-ledger equations. Derive the coordinate a.e. chain rule `d Phi(S_i)/du = sum_e B_ie Z_e'(u) - rho*t'(u)*S_i(S_i-F)/g(S_i)`, **without assuming the transformed equation as the market law**.
- Establish absolute continuity of `H(u)=sum_i Phi(S_i(u))` and of `exp(rho*t(u))*H(u)`; integrate its nonnegative a.e. derivative to propagate the potential barrier.
- Transport positivity, no-short inventory, execution order, bounded variation, exact per-asset terminal closure and matched initial/terminal state through the representation. Prove both the inclusion relevant to every original M1–M5 path and the needed attainable-price interval coverage. Apply localized elasticity/Jensen and already proved exact crisis semantics.
- Only then upgrade the **original manuscript's** fully quantified Theorems 3.1/3.2 to a verified claim. Treat other numbered propositions and the Proposition 7.6 rigorous interval certificate as **separate** proof projects.
