# Results and evidence

Stable LaTeX labels identify results across editions; printed numbering changes in V14. The current manuscript is [V14](../submission/v14-turnover-dissipation/main.tex); V12 in `paper/main.tex` is the original computational/formal baseline. [Version reference](VERSIONS.md).

## Evidence classes

Analytical proofs are mathematical arguments in the manuscript. Lean proofs are kernel-checked declarations with exact hypotheses. Rigorous computer-assisted interval verification uses outward rounding and exact inputs for specified paths. Ordinary numerical/symbolic regression checks selected calculations. Internal review and historical CI are not independent external audits. Open cells or research questions do not assert impossibility.

## Certificate protocols and limitations

The original Proposition 7.6 protocol uses target bands +/-3% and compensator bands +/-25%, two 20-unit sessions, F=eta=1 and rho=0.4. Its certificate establishes C_0.9(40)>0.020, rather than an optimal rate or a general forecast. [Original scope](VERIFICATION_SCOPE.md).

V14 main-table verification uses [loss_breadth_certified.py](../verification/loss_breadth_certified.py) and six paths with scale 10^24. The separately retained twelve-control package uses [verify_all.py](../verification/loss_breadth/verify_all.py), [exact controls](../verification/loss_breadth/phase4_controls.json), [input hashes](../verification/loss_breadth/SHA256SUMS.json) and [result manifest](../verification/loss_breadth/verified_results_manifest.json). See [methodology](../verification/loss_breadth/README.md). Fixed M<3 and relaxed M<4, M<5, M<7 paths must not be pooled as fixed-budget successes. Optional `--audit` is floating-point reconstruction, not a second rigorous proof. OPEN cells are unresolved.

Original accepted source and immutable archive identities remain preserved; the Proposition 7.5 amendment is documented in [A01 audit](../formal/A01_SCOPE_AUDIT.md). Full Lean acceptance is a source/quantifier claim about the original sixteen families, not V14 coverage or a historical-PDF equivalence claim.
