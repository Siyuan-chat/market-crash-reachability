## Evidence and modeling caveats

- JPX2026 documents venue-specific real price-limit rules; the paper's repeated symmetric +/-25% band is an idealized control constraint, not a claim about JPX rules.
- BressanRampazzo1988 contextualizes graph-completed impulse conventions, but does not prove market trade-order independence.
- AlmgrenChriss2001 and Yang2019 contextualize execution under rate restrictions, not market-level reachability optimality.
- BankBaum2004 and CetinJarrowProtter2004 discuss large traders and liquidity-sensitive prices; the activity-clock law is not presented as an equivalent economic model.
- BrunnermeierPedersen2005 concerns strategic predatory trading, which is deliberately absent in the deterministic existence construction.
